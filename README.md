# Icarus

A one-page flight over the sea. Climb as high as the wax will bear. The sun, the birds, and the water are how a flight ends.

**[Play Icarus](https://galactus06.github.io/icarus/)**

![Hangar](eportfolio/hangar.png)

## How to fly

Hold Space, or press and hold on the sky. Let go and he falls. The world moves forward on its own.

Height is what fills the purse. Near the waves the score crawls. Higher up it grows much faster. When the flight ends, that score is added to your purse. Spend it in the hangar before the next takeoff. A bought pair lasts one flight. Unspent money stays. The purse lives in this browser only.

Nothing on the wings tells you how much they can bear. The wax shines when the sun is too close. Gulls tear it. Something in the sea can reach if you stay low. The sky changes while you fly: heat, rain, cloud, wind, and night. A pale column can lift him without your hand, and it leans toward the sun.

## Run it locally

The game is plain HTML, CSS, and one canvas script. There is no build step and no install.

```bash
python -m http.server 8766
```

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8766/
