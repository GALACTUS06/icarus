# How to fly

[Play Icarus](https://galactus06.github.io/icarus/). The first visit opens these lessons on the hangar. After that, **How to fly** brings them back. Nothing in the corner of the sky names a height, a limit, or a count of damage. The score and the purse are the only numbers on the flight.

![The hangar](eportfolio/hangar.png)

## Hold to climb

Hold Space, or press and hold on the sky. Let go and he falls. The world moves forward on its own.

Touch the water and the flight is over. Stay near the waves and something down there can rise for him. If you never climb, that is where he goes.

## Height fills the purse

The score grows as he travels. Near the waves it crawls. Higher up it grows much faster. When the flight ends, that score is added to your purse.

The purse stays in this browser. A new browser, or a private window, starts again from nothing.

## The wings

You begin with basic wax. Spend the purse in the hangar before you fly. Switching pairs before takeoff gives back what you have not yet spent in the air.

| Pair | Price | What the hangar says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic wax | Free | A first pair. It will carry you into the middle sky. |
| Gull | 200 | Climbs easily, and a little more sky feels welcome. |
| Reinforced wax | 560 | Heavier on the climb. The wax holds longer. |
| Daedalus's spare | 1,400 | Strong climb, and the most time near the sun. |

A bought pair lasts one flight. The sea, the things in it, a bird, or the sun destroys the pair you bought. Unspent money remains, and the basic wings are always there. There is no refund after a crash.

## The wings fail suddenly

Stay high and the sky warms. The wax shines. Then the wings are gone, all at once, and he falls. Nothing tells you the height a pair can bear, and the sun does not stay still.

![The wings gave out](eportfolio/wings-gave-out.png)

## Birds

Gulls cross the path. Hit one and it falls. Feathers come off your wings too. The wings look worse and climb worse. Enough tears and they come apart, all at once. Nothing counts the damage for you.

They also cross in a loose scatter, not a single stacked line. The gaps are wide enough to slip through. Catch one and that gull falls, and the wax tears. The others keep flying.

## Columns

A pale column of rising air lifts him even when you let go, and it leans toward the sun. That is a free climb in a direction you did not choose. Ride it, or slip out before the wax shines.

Columns are not copies of each other. Some are short and barely leave the water. Some are thin. Some start higher and reach much farther toward the sun. Staying inside a tall one is the dangerous choice.

## Night

For a while the sun is gone, so the wax rests. Gulls and the things in the sea are only visible when they are close. The score can stay high. The danger is what you cannot see. The dark sky is the warning. Nothing labels the night.

## The sky changes

The weather fades from one spell to the next. You can see each one. Nothing names it while you fly.

- **Heat** hurries the wax. The sun looks larger, and the sky goes gold.
- **Rain** weighs the wings, and they climb less. The sky grays and the rain is bright.
- **Cloud** hides the sun and brings more gulls.
- **Wind** shoves from whichever way it is blowing.
- **Night** puts the sun out.
